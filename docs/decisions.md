# Decisions and open questions for the owner

Items marked **⚑ needs confirmation** are implemented with the default shown but are the owner's call.

## Mechanism

| # | Topic | Implemented | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **⚑ Lot reduction order (FIFO vs LIFO)** | **FIFO** (oldest lot reduced first), as SPEC §2 says. `LOT_REDUCTION_ORDER=lifo` switches the indexer, and `applyDecrease(..., "lifo")` supports it. | The spec itself asks for confirmation. LIFO favours long-term holders: selling recent buys keeps old lots at 1.0×. |
| 2 | **⚑ What a sell consumes first when a wallet holds tokens without lots** (e.g. received by transfer, or the creator's dev-buy tokens) | Those **untracked tokens first**, then lots in FIFO order. | Holder-friendly. Selling transferred tokens doesn't destroy earned age. It cannot be gamed, because lots only ever come from buys. The alternative (lots first) is a one-line change in `packages/core/src/lots.ts`. |
| 3 | Hold time resolution | Weight uses **seconds**: `0.1 + 0.9 × min(seconds, 604800)/604800`. | Same curve as the spec's hours formula, continuous instead of stepping each hour. |
| 4 | **⚑ Dust and carries** | Owed amounts below the minimum (default 0.00002 SOL / 0.001 token), or that would leave an empty wallet below rent-exempt, are carried per wallet. Carries of wallets that **stopped holding** expire after **30 days** and return to the pot. | SPEC §5.3–5.4. The expiry stops an ever-growing tail of dust liabilities. Set `PAYOUT_CARRY_EXPIRY_MS=0` to never expire. |
| 5 | Recipients per round | Up to **1024** (program limit 8192). The largest owed amounts are paid first; the rest are carried whole. | Bounds keeper cost per 2-minute round. Every recipient costs ≈ 1/10 of a transaction fee. |
| 5b | **⚑ Payout economics** | Rounds run on the 2-minute clock but are **skipped while their fees would exceed 2%** of what they pay (`PAYOUT_MAX_FEE_BPS`). The pot keeps growing on-chain and is paid by a later round. Per-recipient minimums rise to the same ratio (≈ 0.0000273 SOL). Payout transactions use a cheap priority fee (1000 µlamports/CU). | Without this, a coin with little volume costs the keeper more in fees than its holders receive. The spec's "every 2 minutes" is then the maximum frequency, not a promise for every coin. Set `PAYOUT_MAX_FEE_BPS=10000` to pay whenever anything is owed. |
| 6 | **⚑ Who pays transaction fees** | The keeper wallet pays every crank and payout fee. Round account rent is refunded on close. Nothing is deducted from vaults. | Roughly 0.000005 SOL per 8–12 payouts. Taking an operator fee from the pot would reduce what holders and the floor receive. |
| 7 | TWAP / ATH defaults | Window 600 s, sample ≥ 40 s (16-slot ring), max gap 120 s, `ath_confirm_secs = 0` (SPEC calls confirmation optional). | **Recommend `ath_confirm_secs = 300` on mainnet** (see docs/security.md). All admin-tunable within hard bounds. |
| 8 | Defend price tolerance | Buyback may pay up to **floor × 1.03** per token, fees included. | Without tolerance a defend at spot = floor would always fail, because pump.fun fees push the effective price above spot. |
| 9 | Defend cadence | Cooldown 60 s, max 6 steps per hour (10% of the floor bucket each). | Bounds: cooldown ≥ 30 s, ≤ 60 steps per hour. |
| 10 | $MOAT burns | Keeper burns a coin's whole burn bucket once it holds ≥ 0.1 SOL, at most hourly, 4% slippage on the quote (on-chain cap 5%). | `hold_mint` can be set once. Until then the burn bucket accumulates. |
| 11 | Payout start time | Rounds align to wall-clock multiples of 120 s. The API, frontend countdown and worker share `nextRoundAt`. | Satisfies the "countdown synced with the server schedule" requirement. |

## Architecture

| # | Topic | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| 12 | SOL custody | A **system-owned treasury PDA** `["treasury", mint]` holds the SOL, is the pump.fun creator and is the buyback `user`. `CoinVault` keeps the bucket accounting. This is required by pump.fun's System-transfer payment path. See docs/program.md. |
| 13 | pump.fun account lists | Passed through `remaining_accounts` and built off-chain with the official SDKs. The program pins the security-relevant positions and builds the instruction data. |
| 14 | Fee claiming | Done by the keeper with pump.fun's permissionless sweep and claim instructions, followed by Moatpad `collect_fees` (also permissionless). |
| 15 | **⚑ Emergency pause** | Admin `set_paused` stops `defend` / `payout` / `open_round` / `burn_hold`. It cannot move funds. | A compromised admin could freeze outflows forever. Keep it behind a multisig, or remove it before mainnet if "unstoppable" matters more than an emergency brake. |
| 16 | Snapshot commitment | The merkle root of each round's entries is committed on-chain in `open_round`, and full inputs are published. Claim-based on-chain verification is a later step (SPEC §7.3). |
| 17 | Database | Postgres (PGlite in tests and local dev). Amounts are stored as exact integers (`numeric(20,0)`). |
| 18 | Program framework | Anchor 0.32 / Solana 2.3 toolchain (platform-tools v1.48). Some transitive crates are pinned in `Cargo.lock` so the SBF toolchain (rustc 1.84) builds. |

## Product

| # | Topic | Status |
|---|---|---|
| 19 | **⚑ UI prototype** | `holdfast-launchpad.html` was **not included** with the spec. The frontend follows the spec text, with theme tokens and layout isolated so it can be matched to the prototype once it's shared. |
| 20 | Launch cost | **Resolved: 0.015 SOL, as the spec says.** A typical launch consumes ≈ 0.0148 SOL. Three changes got it there: the vault account shrank from 1234 to 730 bytes; the treasury's token accounts and pump accumulators are created by `prepare_treasury` out of the coin's own fees instead of at launch; and with a lookup table the launch is one atomic transaction that leaves the deposit at 0 (no rent reserve, no refund tx). The split flow (no lookup table) consumes ≈ 0.0150 SOL but needs a 0.00089 SOL refundable reserve on the deposit. Breakdown in `apps/launcher/README.md`. The quote is exact per name/ticker length, and unused SOL is refunded. |
| 20b | Anti-snipe launch | With `LAUNCHER_LOOKUP_TABLE`, create_v2 + init_coin + dev buy run in **one** v0 transaction, so nobody can buy between the coin's creation and the dev buy. The tokens are delivered to the sender in a follow-up transaction (the delivery does not fit the same packet). |
| 20c | Metadata storage | `METADATA_STORAGE=pinata` pins the image and metadata JSON to IPFS (CIDv1 through Pinata, URIs on `IPFS_GATEWAY_URL`). Local storage is for development only, because the metadata would die with our server. |
| 21 | Dev buy | Bought by the deposit address, then **transferred** to the depositor, so it never creates a lot and never earns, as the spec requires. The dev-buy transaction is also recorded as `launch_sig`, which the indexer skips. |
| 21b | **⚑ Moderation** | Built-in blocked terms (child-abuse terms, scam phrases) are refused at `POST /launch` and again by the launcher. Reserved tickers and names (SOL, USDC, $MOAT, …) are accepted but flagged for review. Admins can hide a coin: it stops being listed and its image, text and links are withheld, but it keeps trading on pump.fun and its vault keeps paying holders, because those funds are the holders'. Image hashes can be blocked, and an external classifier webhook is optional. Auto-hide by report count is off by default because it is sybil-prone. | Hate-speech terms are intentionally not shipped in code; add them with `MODERATION_BLOCKLIST_FILE`. For a public launch, configure a real image classifier (CSAM/NSFW). |
| 22 | Legal | Holder revenue sharing may be regulated (SPEC §7.6). Not a code decision. |
| 23 | **Creator incentive** (owner decision, replaces the spec's 0%) | Creators earn **15%** of their coin's fees. The creator's share accrues in the vault (`creator_sol`) and only the creator wallet recorded at launch can claim it (`claim_creator_fees`, from the coin page). That wallet is the deposit's sender unless the launch form names another one; it is written once and cannot change. | pump.fun pays creators part of the trading fees, so creators need a reason to launch here. Most of the fees still work for the coin; the floor and holder buckets keep their no-withdraw guarantee. Claiming is manual, so an unclaimed share simply waits in the vault. |
| 24 | Comments | Wallet-signed (ed25519 over a readable message binding coin, text and time; no account, no transaction). Anyone can comment; holders get a badge with their current weight, the creator a creator badge. Blocked terms are refused, 3 per minute per wallet, reportable, hideable by admins. | A holders-only mode is a small change if spam becomes a problem. |
| 25 | SOL/USD | Pyth Hermes with a CoinGecko fallback, refreshed every minute, shown only when fresh (≤ 10 min). Display only: nothing on-chain or in payouts uses it. | `PRICE_FEED=off` hides the USD toggle. |
| 26 | Watchlist | Kept in the browser (localStorage), not on the server: no account system exists, and a wallet-keyed server list would need signed writes for little gain. | |
| 27 | Telegram notifier | `/watch <wallet>` (payouts, 0.5× and full-weight milestones) and `/coin <mint>` (round results, floor defences, graduation). Messages are claimed in `tg_sent` before sending, so restarts and redelivered notifications never double-send. Events older than 24 h, or from before a chat subscribed, are not sent. A chat that blocks the bot loses its subscriptions. In groups, any member can change the group's subscriptions. | See docs/notifier.md. |
| 28 | **Platform revenue** (owner decision) | The Moatpad treasury earns **10%** of every coin's fees, taken from the floor share. Final split: **40% floor · 25% holders · 15% creator · 10% platform · 10% burn $MOAT**, fixed in the program. The share accrues in `platform_sol`; `sweep_platform_fees` (permissionless, cranked by the keeper at ≥ 0.01 SOL, at most hourly) pays it to `Config::platform_treasury`, which the admin multisig sets with `set_platform_treasury`. No extra fee on trades made through moatpad.fun. | It applies to every trade of a Moatpad coin, wherever it happens. To keep launch rent flat, `CoinVault` keeps one `market` field (bonding curve, then PumpSwap pool) instead of two: 715 bytes, below the earlier 730. The treasury wallet must hold a little SOL (≥ 0.001) so the first sweep is rent-safe. |
| 29 | **Keeper running costs** | The keeper only sends transactions that move money or can move the ATH: fees are collected after the coin trades (at most every `KEEPER_COLLECT_EVERY_SECS`, default 10 minutes), the TWAP is sampled only while the price is above the ATH and for a window + confirmation tail after, and payout rounds no longer collect first. Routine transactions pay `KEEPER_ROUTINE_PRIORITY_MICROLAMPORTS` (1,000); only buybacks pay the full priority fee. With the keeper set as the platform treasury (`deploy/program.sh revenue <wallet>`), the 10% share pays for its transactions and everything above `KEEPER_RESERVE_LAMPORTS` (0.3 SOL) is forwarded to `KEEPER_PROFIT_WALLET`. | The first version cranked every coin every minute forever, about 0.03 SOL per coin per day, mostly on coins nobody trades. A quiet coin now costs nothing; an active one costs a few thousand lamports per trade window, paid out of its own fees. |
